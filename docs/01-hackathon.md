## Tracks
1. Storefront Experience
2. Buyer Experience
3. Merchant Tooling
4. New Ways to Buy (subscriptions, bundles, marketplaces, reselling, programmatic purchasing)
5. **Agentic Commerce** — AI agents discovering, comparing and completing transactions ← *our track*

## Judging criteria (no weights published)
1. Problem clarity and relevance
2. Buyer, seller or merchant impact
3. Prototype execution quality
4. Meaningful AI / Grok Bot agent integration
5. Commerce stack depth
6. Originality and distinctiveness
7. Plausible business or customer value

Luma adds: technical execution, product thinking, AI leverage & autonomy, real-world usefulness, UX, demo quality.
